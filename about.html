About Fern

Your circular. Your recipes. Your family.

Fern is your personal AI for the kitchen. Every week, grocery stores publish a circular telling shoppers what's on sale — and every week, most of that gets ignored because nobody has time to cross-reference a 20-page ad against what to actually cook. Fern flips that: snap a photo of your weekly circular, and Fern's AI builds personalized recipes, a shoppable list, and a full meal plan around what's already discounted, matched to your household's tastes, dietary needs, and family size.

It doesn't matter where you shop. Fern works with any store's circular, blends AI-generated suggestions with your own personal recipe box, and connects to a growing community of food bloggers for even more inspiration.

What makes Fern different

No other app connects what's on sale directly to what you cook. Recipe apps don't know your store's deals. Deal apps don't help you cook. Fern is built specifically to close that gap — a mechanism that's patent-pending (U.S. App. No. 64/067,385).

Free, genuinely

Fern's circular scanning and AI recipes are free to use, no card required. Pro and Pro Max are optional paid tiers that unlock unlimited scans, a shared Family Hub calendar, Alexa voice integration, and a hands-free Personal Shopper for households that want more.
